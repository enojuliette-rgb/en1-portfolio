Activity 1: Drink Coaster

In order to create this coaster, I used Generative AI to answer questions that I came up with as if the AI was Sun WuKong. The AI answered questions such as what design Sun WuKong would like for a drink coaster, what beverages they enjoy, and other various design related questions. Then, once I had the answers, I used AI to create a coaster using the questions I thought were the most important for the design.
