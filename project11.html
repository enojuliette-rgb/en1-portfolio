Project 11: Haunted House Project

For this project, we wanted to make a snake jump out using a scissor lift. We learned that the snake was going to be too heavy for the Legos to move, so we transitioned our plan to move a skull up and then back down. The skull had glowing eyes that we rigged up inside of the skull, and it was attatched via two rings that connected to an axle that connected two seperate scissor lifts.
