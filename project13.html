Project 13: Fruit Scanner

In order to make this scanner, I used a color sensor to detect the color of a fruit. Then, I coded the model so I could train it myself, and the hub stores the rgb values, and the position of the motor, left being apple, right being banana. I then used the hub buttons to change the mode of the scanner to inference mode. Then, it would use k-nearest neighbors to classify the fruit. Once it classified the fruit, then it would turn the motor to the corresponding direction, and print either "apple" or "banana" based on the direction of the motor.
